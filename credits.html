Rendering credits and provenance

The presentation renderer uses recorded native vehicle poses. Rendering assets do not supply vehicle dynamics or traffic behavior.

V2 replays one native five-vehicle traffic episode with scripted evaluation feedback and one logged local Qwen 9B interpretation. A/B are forecast candidates evaluated by the scripted driver; the final execution plan is recomputed after two feedback updates. This display is a simulation demonstration. The V2 replay provenance identifies the source records. The V1 video and brief remain archived and describe V1.
